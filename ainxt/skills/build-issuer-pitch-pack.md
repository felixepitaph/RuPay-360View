# build-issuer-pitch-pack

Build a cited evidence pack for an issuer conversation.

## Sections
1. Portfolio today (from SQL): CIF, active rate, spend mix by rail, top categories, trend.
2. Proposition comparison: the issuer's RuPay vs Visa/Mastercard variants on fees, earn rates, forex, lounge, exclusions (catalogs, with sources).
3. Offer intensity: offers live in the last 90 days by network and category.
4. What worked: confirmed findings and playbook entries relevant to this issuer.
5. Deal scenarios: base / conservative / aggressive from `r360_deal_engine` with the proposed terms.
6. Asks and next steps (draft for the analyst to edit).
Every number cites its tool call; every proposition fact cites its source.
