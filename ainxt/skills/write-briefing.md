# write-briefing

Write the short plain-English note for a finding, for the Insight Briefing and the weekly email.

## Rules
- 2 to 4 sentences. Lead with what moved and by how much, then the likely cause, then the strongest piece of evidence, then one caution if the Critic gave one.
- Use only numbers that appear in the evidence bundle, written the same way (rounding allowed to whole percent or 2 decimals of lakh/crore).
- Say "likely" or "consistent with" unless confidence is HIGH and the Critic passed it.
- Name the source of the cause (offer ID or catalog event and its document).
- No recommendations unless the playbooks wiki has a confirmed one for this pattern; then add one line starting "Playbook:".

## Example
"Blinkit spend on IDFC FIRST cards fell 25% in the week of 4 Jan (Rs15.6 L vs Rs20.9 L expected). This is likely due to IDFC's Rs150-off Zepto offer (1-7 Jan): most of the spend Blinkit's regular IDFC customers stopped spending there reappeared at Zepto, and other banks' cards at Blinkit were flat. Caution: one-week effect; check whether it reverses after the offer ends."
