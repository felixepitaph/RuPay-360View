# Wiki: glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CVP | Customer value proposition: what a card promises (rewards, milestones, lounges, benefits). |
| MITC | Most Important Terms and Conditions: fees, charges, interest, exclusions. |
| FS / EMI | Full swipe (pay in one go) vs converted to instalments. |
| CIF | Cards in force. |
| Active rate | Active cards / CIF over a window (30/60/90 days). |
| ATS, TPAC, SPAC | Average ticket size; transactions per active card; spend per active card. |
| UPI CC | RuPay credit card used on UPI. |
| Wallet share | Share of a customer's spend that goes to this card vs other cards vs bank-account UPI. |
| Flag | A Watcher alert: a statistically unusual weekly move. |
| Finding | A flag plus candidate causes, evidence tests, cautions and confidence (the evidence bundle). |
| Recapture share | Of the spend a merchant's regular cardholders stopped spending there, the share that appeared at the competitor. |
| DiD | Difference-in-differences: change for affected cards minus change for comparable unaffected cards. |
| Proposal | A suggested change (catalog, metric, threshold, playbook) waiting in the Curator Queue. |
| Net BPS | Deal net revenue / total spends x 10,000 (deal engine). |
